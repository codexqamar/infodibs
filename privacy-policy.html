Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 5, 2026

1. Introduction

DIBS (Digital Image Biometric Systems) ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our biometric-secured media protection application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Biometric Data

  • Face recognition data captured during enrollment and verification
  • Liveness detection data to prevent spoofing attacks
  • Facial feature embeddings stored securely in encrypted format
  • This data is used exclusively for identity verification and vault access control

2.2 Account Information

  • Full name and email address
  • User ID and device identifiers
  • Authentication credentials (passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text)

2.3 Media Files

  • Images and videos you upload to the secure vault
  • Media metadata including file type, size, and upload timestamp
  • All media files are encrypted at rest and in transit

2.4 Access and Activity Logs

  • Timestamps of vault access attempts
  • Verification status (verified or blocked)
  • Device information and platform details
  • Actions performed within the app

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your identity using biometric verification
  • Control access to your protected media vault
  • Prevent unauthorized viewing, sharing, or manipulation of your content
  • Maintain audit trails for security and compliance purposes
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent access attempts
  • Enable offline access to previously verified content
  • Improve app security and user experience

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All biometric data is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption
  • Face embeddings are stored locally on your device and in secure cloud storage
  • Media files are encrypted before upload and remain encrypted at rest
  • Access logs are retained for security auditing and compliance
  • We implement physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect your data
  • Data transmission uses TLS/SSL encryption protocols

5. Data Retention

  • Biometric enrollment data: Retained until you delete your enrollment or account
  • Media files: Retained until you explicitly delete them from your vault
  • Access logs: Retained for 12 months for security and compliance purposes
  • Account information: Retained until account deletion is requested
  • Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations, court orders, or law enforcement requests
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users
  • With service providers who assist in app operation (under strict confidentiality agreements)

We never share biometric data for marketing or advertising purposes.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in the app
  • Export your user data in portable format
  • Request deletion of your biometric enrollment and account
  • Revoke consent for data processing (may limit app functionality)
  • Object to automated decision-making based on biometric data
  • File a complaint with relevant data protection authorities

8. Children's Privacy

DIBS is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect biometric or personal information from children. If we discover such data has been collected, it will be promptly deleted.

9. Biometric Data Consent

By enrolling your face in DIBS, you provide explicit, informed consent for:

  • Collection and storage of your facial biometric data
  • Use of this data for identity verification purposes
  • Storage of encrypted biometric templates on device and in secure cloud

You may revoke this consent at any time by resetting your Face ID enrollment from the Settings menu. This will permanently delete your biometric data.

10. Offline Access

When offline access is enabled, your biometric templates and recently accessed media may be cached locally on your device. This allows vault access without internet connectivity. Re-verification is required periodically and when reconnected online.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification or email. Continued use of DIBS after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, contact us at:

Email: privacy@dibsapp.com
Support: support@dibsapp.com
