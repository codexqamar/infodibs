Working capabilities for cyber-guarded media.

Core features are designed to reduce unauthorized viewing, misuse, manipulation, and untraceable sharing of sensitive content.

Face verification

Media only becomes visible after facial biometric verification confirms the authorized user.

Liveness detection

Verification requires live presence signals to make photo or replay spoofing harder.

Blurred vault

Thumbnails remain visually protected until access is granted.

Protected viewer

Sensitive media is viewed inside a guarded app surface with screenshot prevention on supported mobile devices.

Watermark overlay

Viewed content carries a visible DIBS protection marker for deterrence and accountability.

Access logs

Verified and blocked attempts are recorded to support traceability.
