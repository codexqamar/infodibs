DIBS stands for Digital Image Biometric Systems.

DIBS is built for sensitive media that should not be visible, shareable, or trace-free without verified user identity.

SSystems
Combines vault, viewer, logs, and guardrails into one flow.

What DIBS does.

DIBS creates a stricter path from storage to viewing: identity first, access second, accountability always.

Prevents casual exposure

Vault previews stay locked and blurred until authorized verification succeeds.

Raises spoofing resistance

Liveness detection adds a presence check before media is revealed.

Supports safer viewing

Protected viewer controls discourage screenshots, screen recording, exports, and unapproved redistribution.

Creates accountability

Audit logs make access attempts visible, including blocked attempts and device context.
